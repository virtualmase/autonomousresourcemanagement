Observe the resource before it becomes an exposure.

EXECUTIVE QUESTIONWhat is being consumed, changed, disclosed, or committed—and what signal tells us that the context has changed?
02ARESOURCE
FIELD

A workflow cannot protect what it cannot see.

Observability is not a dashboard ornament. It is a set of conditions that makes resource use, limits, dependencies, and drift available to the person who needs to decide whether work may continue. Start with the resources that would make a seemingly small action material.

01 / SCOPE

Resource boundary

Identify data, funds, time, tools, people, commitments, and downstream dependencies that the workflow could affect.

02 / CONDITION

Operating limit

State the permitted range, expiry, owner, and condition under which a routine action should stop being routine.

03 / SIGNAL

Drift evidence

Define what can reveal a breach, uncertainty, conflict, or changed downstream consequence in time to intervene.

04 / REVIEW

Visible consequence

Keep enough evidence to explain what resource changed, who was affected, and whether a follow-up decision is required.
