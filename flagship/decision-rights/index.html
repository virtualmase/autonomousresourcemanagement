EXECUTIVE QUESTIONWhat may this workflow recommend, execute within a limit, approve, or never touch?
01ARIGHTS
BRIEF

Capability travels farther than authority.

A system can rank alternatives without having permission to make a commitment. It can draft a response without sending it. It can flag an exception without resolving it. Treat these as different rights with different resource and accountability consequences.

01 / RECOMMEND

Propose

Generate or rank an option for review. The output is advice, not an authorization, even when it appears operationally useful.

02 / EXECUTE

Act within a limit

Perform a defined action only within a named resource scope, condition, and owner-approved limit.

03 / APPROVE

Commit

Create a material obligation, alter rights, or accept a consequence. This requires an explicit accountable decision owner.

04 / ESCALATE

Hold and route

Pause when evidence is uncertain, the scope is exceeded, a policy condition conflicts, or another person’s options would change.

OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Do not infer approval from access. If the right is not stated, the safe action is to hold the work for accountable review.
