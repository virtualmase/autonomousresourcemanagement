MODEL

Governing work is not a final approval. It is an operating cycle.

A bounded workflow needs a way to enter with a stated purpose, operate inside declared limits, surface changed conditions, hold when an exception occurs, and learn from a reviewable outcome. The operating model is the link between a useful capability and a consequence someone can actually own.

01 / FRAME

Declare the work

State the outcome, scope, resources, dependencies, time horizon, and the decision that remains with a person.

02 / OPERATE

Apply bounded authority

Allow recommendations or defined actions only where an owner, limit, evidence expectation, and exception condition are explicit.

03 / HOLD

Route a changed condition

When evidence conflicts, scope expands, a limit is approached, or rights would change, stop ordinary progression and surface the decision.

04 / REVIEW

Learn from the record

Inspect outcome and unresolved uncertainty; refine the boundary without rewriting the fact that a prior decision occurred.

MANAGEMENT TEST

If a person cannot name the resource, right, record, escalation owner, and review point, the work may be automated—but it is not yet governed.
