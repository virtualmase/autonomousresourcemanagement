The ARM capability system does not represent autonomous job titles or an invented team roster. It defines responsibility boundaries for evidence, writing, testing, implementation, participation, continuity, and cleanup. Each capability produces a concrete record and hands work forward under review.

R / 01

Researcher

Bounds claims with evidence, names uncertainty, preserves citations, and escalates material evidence gaps before publishing.

C / 02

Content developer

Turns approved evidence into reader-specific material without expanding claims, inventing proof, or obscuring limits.

P / 03

Prototype

Tests an uncertainty with the smallest reversible demonstration and preserves the question, observation, and stop rule.

B / 04

Builder

Implements reviewed work with scope checks, validation, change record, and rollback boundary; it does not self-authorize production changes.

G / 05

Grower

Connects useful material to relevant audiences through transparent distribution, measured learning, and non-deceptive participation.

M / 06

Maintainer

Preserves availability, integrity, contributor clarity, release discipline, and recovery readiness over time.

S / 07

Sweeper

Removes stale, broken, unsafe, or redundant material while keeping provenance, retention obligations, and rollback ability.

PARTICIPATION BOUNDARY

The full charters and contribution paths are public records. Review a capability, raise a bounded issue, or join a Discussion; no contributor is asked to accept hidden authority or opaque automation.
