What is AI Claim Architecture?

AI Claim Architecture is the discipline of mapping a material AI-product statement to the evidence, qualification, authority boundary, reviewer, and next decision that allow the statement to travel responsibly.

Smallest useful record

  1. Exact claim
  2. Current source
  3. Evidence state
  4. Qualification and authority boundary
  5. Owner and next verification action

A claim map is a decision aid. It is not a certificate, legal conclusion, security test, or outcome guarantee.

Why it exists

A confident sentence can outrun its proof.

Agentic products combine marketing language, product documentation, demos, policies, runtime controls, and buyer interpretation. Those surfaces can change at different speeds. A statement that was once narrow may become broader as it is repeated by sales material, partners, search systems, and AI answers.

Claim architecture makes that path inspectable. It asks what is actually being said, which source supports it, what remains unknown, who can authorize the statement, and what evidence would change the decision.

Evidence states

Supported

The supplied source directly supports the bounded statement being reviewed.

Needs verification

The statement is plausible, but its current source path is incomplete or self-reported.

Contradicted / risk

The available material conflicts with the statement or makes its current wording unsafe.

Not assessable

The source boundary does not permit a responsible conclusion.

Review sequence

From language to an accountable decision.

Observe

Record the exact public statement and the surface where it appears.

Map

Attach reviewable sources, currency dates, permissions, and limitations.

Constrain

State what the evidence cannot establish and which authority remains human.

Decide

Assign the next verification action, accountable owner, and review date.
