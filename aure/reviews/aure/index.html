Decision memo

What can responsibly travel now.

The reviewed sources support describing AURE as a public assessment and evidence-decision framework with a proposed standard and explicit limitations. They do not support describing it as an independent certifier, a security or compliance assessor, a continuously operating monitoring service, or evidence of customer outcomes.

30-day verification backlog

  1. Add independent reviewer attribution when an external review exists.
  2. Publish a versioned correction and challenge channel.
  3. Attach reproducible release evidence to material runtime statements.
  4. Keep commercial, certification, security, and compliance conclusions outside the public score.

Correction path

A source owner may challenge a row by identifying the exact statement, current public source, and requested correction. A challenge is evidence intake—not an automatic change or endorsement.
