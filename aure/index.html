AURE Portfolio Operator / Control Record

Autonomy without theater.

A public record for policy-bound portfolio work. This surface distinguishes what has been observed, what is authorized, what is blocked, and what remains paused. It does not turn a completed observation into an unverified outcome claim.

Production surface

Flagship subdomain on Hostinger; public routing, TLS, and the published operator control record have been verified.

Execution posture

Policy-bound. No irreversible, financial, legal, deceptive, or unverified-claim actions are authorized here.

Evidence discipline

Every state needs a source, an authorization boundary, and a recorded verification or explicit blocker.

01Operating boundary

A constrained operator, not an outcome generator.

The operator may assemble evidence, check ready-state conditions, maintain an append-only record, and execute only explicitly authorized, reversible actions. It must stop when a source is missing, credentials fail, a workstream is paused, an action is duplicated, or a claim cannot be verified.

Policy core / AURE-AOP-0.2

Authority follows the evidence chain.

Site deployment, content release, connector use, and portfolio maintenance are separate authority domains. A passing health check is never implied permission to publish, message, spend, alter a third party, or make a public performance claim.

  • Blue / observed: source or validation record exists; no outcome is inferred.
  • Brass / blocked: a specific external precondition prevents further action.
  • Red / paused: an owner directive or hard-stop forbids action until resumed.

04Fail-safe controls

Fail closed when authority is uncertain.

A working control is not a button graphic. The authoritative pause control is deployed in the authenticated AURE execution layer and is durable, logged, and checked before every action. This static publication surface intentionally cannot mutate any workstream.

Global hard-stop / required invariant

Execution pause is held outside this page.

External calls are disallowed whenever a workstream is paused, authorization is absent, source evidence is incomplete, credentials are invalid, the action is irreversible, a duplicate key is present, or a stated claim lacks verification. Resume must be recorded by the authenticated policy owner.
