Contribute or request a correction

A practical route for improving definitions, citations, examples, and industry coverage.

Updated 2026-10-15

On this pageSuggest a factual correctionContribute an articleSuggest industry coverageHow changes are reviewed

Suggest a factual correction

Open a correction request with the page URL, the exact passage, a proposed correction, and a supporting source. State whether the issue is a factual error, outdated information, unclear wording, or a missing perspective.

GitHub requires an account to submit an issue. Please omit private operational data, credentials, and personal information from public reports.

Contribute an article

Use the public repository to propose a Markdown article or an edit. Explain the topic's relevance, cite original sources, and distinguish sourced facts from your own examples and analysis.

Disclose relevant affiliations. State whether AI assisted with drafting or verification. Do not invent review credentials or imply that a cited project endorses this site.

Suggest industry coverage

Suggest a source or story. Include the original announcement or research, its date, and the resource-management question it affects. A suggestion does not guarantee coverage.

How changes are reviewed

Submissions are checked for factual support, neutral wording, relevant scope, and clear limitations. Technical checks validate internal links and required article metadata. Passing those checks is not a substitute for editorial judgment.

The maintainer decides whether to publish. Material corrections are recorded in the revision log, and each article links to its source history.
