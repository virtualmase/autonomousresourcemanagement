# Code of Conduct

This project exists to support rigorous, respectful work on accountable resource decision systems. Participants are expected to engage in good faith, distinguish evidence from opinion, respect privacy and security boundaries, and make room for informed disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, impersonation, publication of private information, and attempts to misuse collaboration channels are not permitted. Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock content; limit participation; or escalate serious conduct concerns through the private contact named in `SUPPORT.md`.

When you disagree, address the claim, source, or proposed change—not the person. When a conclusion is uncertain, state uncertainty rather than overstating confidence.
