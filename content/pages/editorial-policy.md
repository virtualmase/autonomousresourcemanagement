## Purpose and scope
Autonomous Resource Management is an editorially maintained knowledge resource about automated allocation, orchestration, infrastructure, and oversight. Coverage is selected for its relevance and evidence, not for its ability to refer readers to a commercial provider.

## Neutral point of view
Describe what a source demonstrates and what remains uncertain. Attribute disputed claims. Give approaches attention proportional to their relevance and supporting evidence; neutrality does not require giving unsupported claims equal weight.

Separate established technical concepts, the site's proposed framework, and dated industry developments. Examples and recommendations must be labeled as editorial guidance unless directly supported by cited evidence.

## Source standards
Prefer original specifications, project documentation, public research, and official announcements for factual descriptions. An official announcement establishes what its publisher says; it does not independently validate performance or market leadership.

For claims of effectiveness, seek reproducible evidence and independent evaluation where available. Identify preprints, vendor benchmarks, and conflicts of interest. Cite evidence near the claim and explain each source's scope in the references.

## News and review
News briefs distinguish the date of the source event from this site's publication date. Each brief explains what changed, why it may matter, and the limits of the evidence. Older developments may be covered as background; they must not be presented as breaking news.

Automated discovery places candidates in an editorial queue. It does not publish articles. Before publication, an editor checks the original source, dates, attribution, scope, related wiki entries, and correction needs. The publication record identifies whether the source check was human or AI-assisted. Do not imply independent testing or expert review when neither occurred.

## AI assistance
AI may assist with discovery, drafting, source comparison, and maintenance. Launch articles disclose AI-assisted source synthesis. No invented human reviewer, interview, test result, or credential may be added. A maintainer remains responsible for accepting and correcting published content.

## Ownership and conflicts
The site is maintained by Mason Nguyen. The previous homepage linked to commercial implementation properties; those referral paths have been removed from the reference site's navigation and content model. This change does not establish independent ownership or erase relevant affiliations.

Contributors must disclose a relevant commercial relationship when covering an affiliated product or organization. The site does not sell favorable coverage, rankings, or placement in its reference articles. Author attribution is retained.

## Corrections and revision dates
Report a correction through the [contribution page](/contribute/). Include the URL, disputed passage, and evidence. Material factual corrections receive a visible note and a revision-history entry. Update dates change for substantive edits, not to create an appearance of freshness.

## Reuse and attribution
Summarize sources in original language and link to the original. Do not reproduce full third-party articles or images without permission. Source code visibility does not grant rights to third-party material. This site makes no blanket open-content licensing claim.
